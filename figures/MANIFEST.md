Rewritten 2026-09-26. **The previous MANIFEST described an earlier round of the
analysis and contradicted the shipped results in eight specific places** — it is
listed as finding F4 in [../VERIFICATION.md](../VERIFICATION.md). Every number
below was read back out of `../stats/` at packaging time.

Panel = **28 analytes** (>80% above background). All measures are **within-sex
z-scores**, so axes read "(z-score)" and bars may go below zero. Correlations
are **free-permutation Spearman** (B = 50,000) on the z-scored table, with BH
q as the primary multiplicity control and Westfall–Young as the strict
cross-check. 300 dpi PNG + vector PDF + editable SVG (svglite, for Illustrator)
for every panel. Canvases are drawn at FIG_SCALE = 0.60 of their nominal size so
text fills more of each panel (03_theme.R).

Panel 4A (the BioRender paradigm schematic) is not generated by this code.

## Figure 2 and Supplementary Figure 4 are one figure on two genotypes

Both come from a single definition in `04_panels.R`, so they cannot drift apart
in style again. Supp 4A adds the Abeta 1-42 and Abeta 1-40 annotation columns
the published 4A carries, on ramps sampled from that panel's own colour bars.

**The palette is the published manuscript's own hexes** (`00_setup.R`):
NoFlicker `#E8A941`, 20Hz `#5C81BD`, 40Hz `#C85EAD` for WT. New panels are meant
to sit beside untouched ones without looking foreign. The known trade-off is
recorded in `00_setup.R`: 20 Hz and 40 Hz separate by only ΔE 3.8 under
protanopia, and NoFlicker sits at 2.0:1 contrast on white. Identity is therefore
**never carried by colour alone** anywhere in this pipeline — sex is point
**shape**, and groups are direct-labelled or faceted.

**The violins carry no statistics.** The published panels print p-values and
brackets on them; those are tests on a latent variable fitted to separate the
groups, so they are circular. The Wood lab asked for them to go
(`initialComments.md:8`). Model credibility is reported instead as LOOCV
accuracy against a label-permutation null, in the scores subtitle, in words.
**Do not add brackets, asterisks or p-values to these panels.**

## Five things a reader will ask about

**1. Fig 4C has exactly one star.** GM-CSF ~ Abeta 1-42, rho = 0.65,
**BH q = 0.030**. Stars follow BH q across the 28-analyte panel, not the raw p
the published panel starred. This cell now *also* passes Westfall–Young
(**p = 0.024**), which it did not before the sex z-score (it failed at p = 0.25).
Read it with its resolution columns in `../stats/corr_cytokine_amyloid.csv`:
**5 distinct values, 33% ties** — worth a line in the legend.

**2. Fig 5C and 5D are empty, and that is the finding.** The analytes are the
5xFAD PLS-DA key cytokines — KC, IP-10, IL-17, LIF, M-CSF, IL-12(p70). Against
astrocyte number nothing comes close (best q = 0.86). Against microglia number
the best are M-CSF and LIF at **q = 0.12** — neither significant. The analytes
that *do* track microglial density (IL-3, IL-15, Eotaxin, IL-10; q = 0.012) are
**none of the key cytokines.** Carrying group separation and correlating with
morphology are different claims, and this figure is where they come apart.

**3. Supp 7 shows the WT key cytokines, and none of them reach q < 0.05.**
Best BH q per target, over the whole 28-analyte family:

| Target | best q |
|---|---|
| Astrocyte tiling (mean distance to nearest neighbour) | 0.681 |
| Microglia tiling (mean distance to nearest neighbour) | 0.656 |
| Astrocyte number | 0.221 |
| Microglia number | 0.989 |

No panel is starred, and each subtitle says so explicitly so "key" is never read
as "significant". The published caption credits IL-10 with correlating to
astrocyte and microglia tiling; that relationship does not survive the sex
z-score. Panels built on ≤ 6 distinct raw values are labelled "[k levels]".

**4. Supp 4's caption does not survive the panel.** It reads "Flicker reduces
expression of IL5 in 5xFAD mice", but IL-5 is one of the four analytes the
>80%-above-background gate drops, so it is not in Supp 4A and cannot be. IL-5 is
the closest call in the whole gate — 34/43 = 0.791 against a 0.80 threshold, one
animal short. Either the caption changes or the gate is argued around in
Methods; the figures cannot settle it.

**5. Supp 6 deliberately does not use the key cytokines.** Every other
downstream figure selects on the PLS-DA. Supp 6 cannot: Supp 5 already shows
exactly those analytes per genotype with the same statistics, so pointing Supp 6
at the same rule would make it a pixel duplicate. Supp 6 instead keeps the
published figure's job — **one** analyte set shown across **both** genotypes, so
WT and 5xFAD can be read against each other — chosen by flicker responsiveness
(smallest omnibus p from stage 06, ranked on the best result across genotypes).
Still data-driven, still cascades, nothing hardcoded. Flagged in `15_supp6.R`
for a decision: keep it, cut it, or swap it for the keys and cut Supp 5.
